Projects

Energy fraud detection

Deep learning · Python, PyTorch

A fraud and anomaly detection pipeline on smart meter data. LSTM autoencoders turn five time series per meter (consumption, outages, alarms, maintenance, contract data) into embeddings, and DNN and tree-based classifiers label each meter as regular, anomalous or fraudulent.

On a heavily imbalanced dataset, a soft-voting DNN ensemble reached 0.57 weighted recall, against 0.03 for the tree-based models.

Reinforcement learning with PandaGym

Real-time red light violation detection

A real-time pipeline that reads the licence plates of cars running red lights, cutting manual monitoring time and cost by 70%.
